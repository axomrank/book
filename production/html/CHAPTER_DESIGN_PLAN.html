SERIES PRODUCTION SYSTEM / VERSION 1

History that is easy to study.

A reusable, image-led HTML-to-PDF system for Assam government exam aspirants.

Native AI conceptual illustration. Not an excavated assemblage or a reconstruction of a named site.

Design direction

Dimensional imagery, quiet reading surfaces, clear fact hierarchy. Maroon anchors the page; ivory groups ideas; gold marks transitions. The supplied Rang Ghar mark remains the brand authority.

English edition first. Preserve accurate Assamese names; a future Assamese edition needs Noto Sans Bengali and fluent editorial review before release.

01 / The repeatable chapter journey

ComponentReader purpose and content
Chapter openerNumber, title, scope, 3–5 study outcomes, route through the chapter and one dimensional conceptual illustration or credited documentary hero.
FoundationsShort numbered explanations, key terms and an Assam connection. Explain unfamiliar words at first use.
Core studyEvidence, chronology, people, places and causes. Use chronological tables when dates are secure; retain ranges and disagreements.
Visual evidenceArtefact plates, maps, excavation sections, coins, inscriptions, archival photographs or document details. Explain what to notice and what the image cannot establish.
Comparison / confusionParallel facts and common confusions, with qualifications kept beside the claim.
Rapid revisionOne or two pages: cue/recall table, essential chronology and key distinctions. Do not claim exam frequency without verified papers.
PracticeOriginal MCQs, statement questions and short-answer frameworks. Answers follow in a separate section with explanation and source/section pointers.
SourcesNumbered, clickable notes with author, work, date, printed page, figure/table and URL. Research gaps stay in the editorial dossier.

02 / Page and type specifications

7 × 10-inch trim; inner margin 0.68 inch, outer 0.58 inch, top/bottom 0.62 inch. Body Noto Sans 10.5 pt at 1.48 line height; tables 9 pt; captions 8 pt; chapter title 31 pt; section headings 16 pt. Never shrink body text to rescue a crowded page.

Use one main text column. Compare two images only when both remain legible. Running heads identify series and chapter; bottom folio is automatic. Book front matter will use Roman numerals and main text Arabic numbers when a complete volume is assembled. This standalone proof uses continuous Arabic numbering.

Start every chapter on a new page; recto openings are optional in the final bound edition. Section headings stay with the following paragraph. Keep table rows, questions and image/caption groups together; repeat table headers when tables continue. Use widows/orphans of three lines. Allow text to flow rather than forcing every topic onto its own page.

03 / Images with depth and a teaching job

Visual3D treatmentEvidence rule
Conceptual openerNative generated floating objects, tactile paper and physically plausible shadows.Label modern illustration. No invented inscriptions, dates, diagnostic tool markings or population identity.
Documentary plateFlat faithful image inside a raised ivory mount. Screen shadows; print-safe offset edge and tonal backing preserve depth in PDF.Never generatively reshape an artefact. Keep source scales and orientation; retain source image separately.
Site mapRelief-inspired backdrop only where a sourced terrain layer supports it. Locations and labels remain crisp.Record present-day vs historical boundaries; no invented site coordinates. Include legend, north and appropriate scale.
Excavation sectionDimensional panel or separate conceptual exploded-layer diagram.A diagram is explicitly schematic; an actual section drawing retains its reported geometry.
Two-object comparisonConsistent mats, lighting and visual spacing, independent captions.Do not imply equal physical size unless scale is normalized from reliable measurements.

Plan one purposeful visual every 2–3 teaching pages when evidence supports it. Prefer a 25–40% page-height plate beside its explanation; use a full page for detailed maps or inscriptions. Image frequency is flexible, never a filler quota. Aim for 300 effective pixels per inch at print size; flag lower-resolution source material rather than inventing detail.

Caption contract

Figure ID → site/object and present-day state → what to notice → evidence limitation → source and exact figure/page → image date if known → credit/license and publication clearance. A research-only image can appear in an internal proof with its status, but cannot silently enter a publication export.

04 / The same system across thirteen books

Keep the page geometry, typography, maroon/ivory palette, caption scheme, revision structure and question/answer pattern fixed. Change the evidence modules: artefacts and contexts for prehistory; attributed narratives and textual variants for mythology; rulers, inscriptions and chronology for Kamarupa; institutions, campaigns and source comparisons for Ahom history; archival documents and movement timelines for colonial and modern books.

Mythology's editorial position does not make it a measured archaeological era. Later dynastic monuments must not be used as prehistoric evidence merely because they look ancient.

05 / HTML is the editable master

Author semantic HTML directly with stable chapter, division, claim and note IDs. Shared book.css supplies screen and paged-media styles. Figure metadata are kept in a JSON asset manifest. Existing Markdown is retained as a legacy snapshot; it is not the continuing export dependency.

Run python render.py INPUT.html OUTPUT.pdf in an environment containing WeasyPrint 70.0 and pypdf. No browser automation is required. The renderer rejects missing local images and failed PDF creation. Review the resulting PDF pages visually after every meaningful design change.

Before publication: finish primary-source checks, confirm sample-to-layer associations, clear imagery, review Assamese where present, verify references and folios, and test the printer's actual colour/binding requirements. A successful proof is layout evidence, not editorial approval.

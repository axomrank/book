## Voice and feel

The guide describes a capable tutor who knows the local context and respects the reader's time. The intended feeling is steady confidence. For history books, use concrete explanations and engaging, evidence-led narrative, with warmth and restraint. Explain unfamiliar offices, communities, calendars, and names when first introduced. Give English and Assamese equal explanatory depth when both editions are requested.

Assamese identity comes through accurate local knowledge, language, subject matter, and the Rang Ghar mark. Avoid exaggerated hero worship, vague grandeur, manufactured drama, shame, guaranteed exam success, and decorative stereotypes. The brand tagline is **Prepare smarter. Rank higher.** Use it only where appropriate to a brand signature, not as the recurring ending of historical chapters.

## Visual baseline

| Role | Value | Book use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Maroon `#80151A` | Brand signature, chapter accents, key headings |
| Learning/editorial background | Warm ivory `#F7F2EC` | Cover panels and digital editorial surfaces |
| Main reading text | Charcoal `#292929` | Body, notes, captions |
| Light contrast | White `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark panels and appropriate paper backgrounds |
| Correct/completed state | Muted green `#3F6B4F` | Labelled learning states when needed |
| Decorative accent | Gold `#A77B37` | Restrained ornament; never small body copy |

Use Noto Sans for English and Noto Sans Bengali for Assamese, with few weights and generous spacing. The kit includes regular/bold fonts with licensing records. Social-card pixel sizes are not book typography. Choose and test print sizes against the actual trim, notes, and language. Avoid shrinking Assamese copy to force a fit; preserve ৰ and ৱ, shaping, and adequate line spacing. Independent fluent Assamese editorial signoff remains pending until recorded.

Use clean editorial composition, generous margins, clear hierarchy, and consistent chapter opening, folio, running head, and caption treatments. Use flat colour and restrained ornament for interior pages. Do not introduce woven/gamosa framing: later saved brand preferences exclude it. Match the selected cinematic 3D cover where the book calls for it while keeping identity, title hierarchy, and reading surfaces coherent.

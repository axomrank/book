**Primary audience:** Assam government examination aspirants. Chapters use structured explanations, fact and comparison tables, revision summaries, source citations and original practice questions.

A planned history of Assam from its earliest recoverable past to the contemporary state: **thirteen books, 222 chapters, and 1,110 numbered content divisions**. The proposed umbrella title is **Assam: A History of Its Land and People**. Revised **11 October 2026**; that date remains the contemporary cutoff.

The revised opening sequence is **prehistory → mythological Assam → Varman → Salastambha → Pala**. Mythology has its own textual and cultural treatment and is not inserted as a dated archaeological era. The existing Ahom trilogy now forms Books **07–09**, with its selected title, original chapter numbers, evidence records, drafts, and cover decisions preserved. Book 06 is the parallel regional companion.

## Mythology and dynasty naming

The mythological book includes **Sura Danava**, the Danava figures, **Naraka**, Bhagadatta, Vajradatta, Bana, Usha, Chitralekha, Kamakhya, and associated traditions. Sura Danava’s precise identification is a stated source-checking task; no biography has been invented. The dynasty books preserve **Barma/Varman**, **Salstambha/Salastambha**, and **Pal/Pala** variants. Bhauma/Naraka ancestry receives dedicated treatment across mythology and dynasty chapters rather than an assumed exclusive synonym for Salastambha.

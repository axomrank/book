# AxomRank illustration collection v1

Target: 36 square AI-generated master illustrations, plus eight editable concept explanations. Commission authorised by Angsuman Das on 11 October 2026 after approving four samples and accepting the house architecture, exterior and vegetation direction.

The four samples were individually approved. The remaining collection was authorised as a batch; its generation and technical review are recorded separately. Do not invent individual owner approval for new scenes. Historical reconstructions still require heritage/editorial signoff before use as factual teaching material.

## Browse and reuse

- Open [gallery.html](gallery.html) for all masters, IDs, classification, alt text and review status.
- masters/ contains 36 original PNG files, organised by six sets.
- records/ contains exact prompts, reference paths and generation provenance.
- production-plan.json documents all 36 briefs and source leads.
- concept-diagrams/ contains editable SVGs with the actual teaching methods. Use these alongside the concept art; generated counts, arrows and wiring are not authoritative.
- evidence/ contains cultural context and historical reconstruction limits.
- manifest.json lists dimensions, checksums, captions, alt text, source records and review status.
- [qa.json](qa.json) records automated checks; [visual-review.json](visual-review.json) records scene review notes.

## Classification and publication

Label realistic scenes **AI-generated illustration** wherever they could be mistaken for documentary photographs. Environment scenes are regional concept art, not evidence of an exact place or current conditions. Historical art must carry **Conceptual reconstruction — not to scale** and its source brief. No real people, credentials, testimonials, scores or app interface are asserted.

House and landscape approval applies to the reviewed visual direction, not to every community in Assam. Brahmaputra Valley, Barak Valley and hill contexts are distinguished. Clothing is ordinary present-day clothing; it is not presented as community costume. Do not infer plant species or exact season from an illustration.

Use existing compositions across multiple lessons. Keep editable Assamese/English text outside the bitmap; leave one idea per card. Add the supplied logo through the existing brand templates after checking its source rights. No invented logo has been generated. Use campaign backgrounds with supplied brand elements as separate editable overlays.

Generation records document the native tool and reference images. They are provenance records, not a claim of exclusive copyright or an executed rights grant. The asset register keeps generation-term review separate from visual approval.

# Evidence briefs for four history teaching illustrations

All four are limited conceptual reconstructions. Required caption: **AI-generated conceptual reconstruction — not to scale; details require heritage review.** They do not reconstruct people, dress, battles or exact historic geography. Do not publish as exact archaeological evidence.

## HT-01 — Rang Ghar

The district page describes a two-storey pavilion with an octagonal base, arched entrances and a roof resembling an inverted boat. The brief uses these broad features only. The source's completion date differs from another official university page (1744/1746); no exact year is inserted in this asset. Ornament, measurements, restored colour and period surroundings are not established. No royal spectators or animal fights generated.

Primary sources: https://sivasagar.assam.gov.in/index.php/tourist-place-detail/276 ; https://argucom.assam.gov.in/frontimpotentdata/about-sivasagar .

## HT-02 — Moidam structure

UNESCO documents mound burials with hollow vaults; its decision text describes the mound, vault, summit shrine and enclosing wall. The illustration is a simplified teaching cutaway of those components. Its proportions, construction sequence and individual vault design are conjectural. No remains, grave goods or religious enactment are shown. Do not present it as an excavation drawing or one named royal burial.

Primary sources: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1711/ ; https://whc.unesco.org/document/206972 .

## HT-03 — Talatal Ghar masonry fragment

The district account describes brick construction, corridors and multiple wings. This brief deliberately limits reconstruction to a small corridor fragment. It does not claim to restore missing upper rooms or map tunnels. No tunnel lengths, floor plan, seven-floor elevation or royal interior are drawn. Broad material/context is sourced; opening dimensions and viewpoint are illustrative.

Primary source: https://sivasagar.assam.gov.in/tourist-place-detail/277 .

## HT-04 — Historic tank landscape

The district account identifies Sivasagar's historic tank and royal commissioning context. The illustration teaches the broad idea of a managed tank landscape. Embankment, step access, vegetation, colour and viewpoint are schematic. It omits temple geometry, construction labour, dates and exact dimensions because these would require a separate measured/evidence brief.

Primary source: https://sivasagar.assam.gov.in/index.php/tourist-place-detail/275 .

## Editorial use

Use the factual source caption alongside art, not a claim that AI recovered a lost historical view. Review broad geometry, missing/invented structures and source limits before using art in a named-monument lesson. The historic tank and corridor are teaching abstractions, not complete palace/site reconstructions. A precise site survey or historic-costume scene remains outside this collection's evidence scope.

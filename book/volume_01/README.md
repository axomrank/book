## Evidence and production status

Two complete historical source scans are stored locally. Gait was read in extracted text; selected pages of Barua’s image-only bilingual edition were read visually in English. Full modern works that could not be acquired are marked as leads, not presented as read. The sources are only a beginning and largely court-centred.

Gait and Barua share part of their source lineage. Population figures and sacred-origin stories are not independently verified events. The Sutuphaa accession, Tyao Khamti dates, Habung duration and some conquest attributions remain openly disputed. Mula Gabharu’s source history has not yet been established here.

No source-verified maps, genealogical plates, object photographs or publication page layout have been completed. The 7 × 10 inch portrait trim remains the master plan’s production recommendation; there is no measured page count for this unfinished volume. The current drafts are shorter than the eventual chapter allocations and will expand as evidence is added.

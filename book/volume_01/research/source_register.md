## G1906 — A History of Assam

**Author/editor:** Edward A. Gait. **Date:** 1906. **Type:** Colonial historical synthesis using translated chronicles and other records.

[Source link](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/02/A_History_of_Assam_by_E._A._Gait.pdf)

**Access:** Downloaded complete PDF; relevant sections read in extracted text; selected page images prepared.

**Actually consulted:** Introduction pp. i–vii; pp. 34–46; pp. 67–102; appendix chronology consulted in OCR but table requires visual rechecking.

**Limits:** Gait did not independently read Tai-Ahom. Translation work involved Barua and Deodhai scholars. Colonial racial explanations rejected. Appendix table OCR is unreliable; narrative pages used for chronology.

## B1930 — Ahom-Buranji, with parallel English translation

**Author/editor:** Golap Chandra Barua, editor and translator. **Date:** 1930. **Type:** Edited Tai-Ahom chronicle with English translation.

[Source link](https://rarebooksocietyofindia.org/book_archive/196174216674_10156417306701675.pdf)

**Access:** Downloaded complete image-only PDF. Selected English-column pages read visually. No usable native text extraction.

**Actually consulted:** Title and contents; unnumbered preface, PDF page 3; printed pp. 24–25, 34–38, 44–50 (PDF pages 30–31, 40–44, 50–56).

**Limits:** Only the English translation has been read. Modern headings and paragraphs were editor-added. This chronicle was used by Gait: agreement is not independent corroboration. Regnal passages contain internal date/duration conflicts.

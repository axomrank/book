# Ahom history books — title shortlist

Prepared 10 October 2026. **Final decision:** the user selected **From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar**. The earlier shortlist below is retained as the decision record. No availability or trademark search has been performed.

Reviewed relevant strategy and brand-guide text preserved in the earlier AxomRank project chat: the September 2026 *Social Media & Brand Strategy*, the October 2026 *Brand Identity Guide*, and the Instagram content strategy. The original files are in that earlier cloud workspace, not in this local book folder. The emotional signature is “Rooted in Assam. Rising with You.” The product tagline is “Prepare Smarter. Rank Higher.” Book naming should follow the credibility and accessibility principles. The exam-product tagline need not become the book title.

## Earlier recommendation — retained for the decision record

**Ahom: A Kingdom and Its People**

Suggested shared subtitle: **A History of the Ahom Dynasty, 1228–1828**.

This gives the subject immediate visibility and leaves room for rulers, institutions, communities, livelihoods and culture. It fits the saved comprehensive content plan and its endpoint at Gomdhar Konwar’s 1828 movement. The subtitle describes the set; individual volumes have different chronological and thematic coverage.

Possible Assamese companion title: **আহোম: ৰাজ্য আৰু মানুহ**. Have the complete Assamese cover wording reviewed by a fluent editorial reader before publication, in accordance with the brand guide’s language standard.

## Three-volume naming system

Keep the shared title on every cover and distinguish the volumes through subtitles:

1. **Volume I — Origins, Foundation and Expansion**. Main political chronology: 1228–1603, with necessary earlier background.
2. **Volume II — War, Power and Renewal**. Main political chronology: 1603–1769.
3. **Volume III — Society, Collapse and Resistance**. Thematic chapters span the kingdom’s history; the principal late political narrative leads through collapse to Gomdhar Konwar in 1828. Do not label all its thematic content as confined to 1769–1828.

Suggested imprint line: **An AxomRank History Publication**. Use “AxomRank” in running text and “AXOMRANK” only where the approved wordmark treatment calls for it. This is a proposed book imprint, not an existing product name established by the strategy.

## Selection criteria

The main title should identify Ahom clearly, remain readable at a small cover size, work when spoken aloud, and suit a serious general reader as well as a learner. Avoid promises such as “every fact” or “the only complete history”: the research plan explicitly retains uncertainty and cannot demonstrate exhaustive inclusion of every internet statement. Retain a shared series identity across all three volumes.

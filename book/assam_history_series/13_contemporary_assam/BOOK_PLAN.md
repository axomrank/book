# Assam Today in Historical Perspective: Society, Politics, and Change

**Series position:** Book 13 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1985–11 October 2026; fixed edition cutoff, not a live news feed.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

Provide a dated historical account through **11 October 2026**, not forecasts or an automatically current encyclopaedia. Cover policies, elections, rights, livelihoods, cultural production, environmental risks, and regional differences. All contemporary claims require their own source cutoff; this plan does not establish the current outcome of any legal case or policy.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **55,000–80,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Government, armed conflict, rights, and civilian life:** Chapters 1–4.
- **Autonomy, citizenship, borders, and migration:** Chapters 5–8.
- **Industry, rural livelihoods, cities, and environmental risk:** Chapters 9–12.
- **Conservation, social services, gender, and culture:** Chapters 13–16.
- **Youth, pandemic, the 2020s, and a dated state portrait:** Chapters 17–20.

Planning check: **11 October 2026**. These are acquisition routes and limited consulted summaries, not a claim that every proposed chapter has been researched. No chapter has been newly fact-checked or drafted by this planning task.

## Additional acquisition work

Find exact epigraphic editions and excavation reports; edited regional chronicles; Persian and Burmese witnesses; Gazette and treaty texts; district records and newspapers; census tables with matching geography; judicial judgments and official election returns; and peer-reviewed historical, linguistic, environmental, and social studies. None of these unnamed works is represented as already read. Record title, author/editor, edition, composition date, copy date, page/folio, access route, language, source lineage, consulted passages, and rights in each book’s research records when acquired.

## Additional leads for the revised mythology and dynasty books

- Location: [Jae-Eun Shin — Changing Dynasties, Enduring Genealogy](https://list.indology.info/pipermail/indology/attachments/20170209/d815b926/attachment.pdf).

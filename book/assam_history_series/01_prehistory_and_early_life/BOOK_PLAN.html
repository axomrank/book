Before the Kingdoms: Prehistory and Early Life in Assam

Series position: Book 01 of Assam: A History of Its Land and People (proposed umbrella title).

Coverage: Earliest recoverable evidence to c. fourth century CE; site-specific chronologies.

Status: Chapter 01 is a provisional study draft; expanded regional research must precede further chapter drafting. HTML is the continuing plan and manuscript format.

Scope and relationship to the set

Reconstruct the deep past from recoverable evidence. The endpoint marks a change in available evidence, not the sudden end of prehistoric practices or the appearance of a modern state. Proposed site interpretations and chronology require research before narrative drafting.

Audience, language, scale, and brand

Assam government exam aspirants. Use short structured explanations, comparisons, revision tables and original practice questions. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the shared editorial and brand system.

Working main-text allocation: 30,000–45,000 words, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

Chapter divisions

- Evidence and landscapes: Chapters 1–3. - Early communities and archaeological sites: Chapters 4–6. - Food, crafts, and connections: Chapters 7–9. - Ritual, transition, and knowledge limits: Chapters 10–12.

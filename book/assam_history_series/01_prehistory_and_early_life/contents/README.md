The page includes twelve canonical chapter titles in four thematic groups, read down the left column (01–06), then down the right (07–12). Typography and illustration were generated together with the built-in image-generation tool. Visual inspection confirmed titles, numbering and grouping against `../chapter_plan.json`.

Page numbers are omitted until manuscript pagination. This is a raster design proof, not a final typeset or press-ready contents page. The small jar is a modern decorative illustration. It matches the user's selected v6 cover in palette, serif typography and paper treatment.

                                                                ABSTRACT
                   Trivedi A., Phukan A., Nag A., Gogoi B. Dehingia P. Rao S.V. & Harmukh N. 2025. Holocene vegetation
               dynamics and climate variability inferred from pollen records from Northeast India. Geophytology 55(1): 45–54.

                      We report the preliminary findings of a palynological investigation conducted on a 50 cm long sediment core
               retrieved from a swamp within the Gauhati University Campus, Kamrup Metropolitan District, Guwahati, Assam,
               Northeast India for understanding the vegetation dynamics. The palynological assemblage reveals a rich diversity
               of pollen taxa including tropical moist deciduous taxa, marshy taxa, highland species, anthropic indicators and
               ferns. Based on the relative abundances these pollen taxa, three distinct pollen zones GU-I, GU-II and GU-III
               have been identified. The presence of tropical moist deciduous taxa including Syzygium cumini, Shorea robusta,
               Lagerstroemia in the first zone suggest that a moist and more humid climate prevailed throughout the period of
               sediment accumulation. The presence of highland taxa such as Abies, Pinus and Alnus indicates wind activity
               facilitating the transport of pollen grain from higher elevations. Furthermore, the higher frequencies of cultural
               pollen taxa such as Poaceae and Cerealia in Pollen Zone GU-I and GU-II reflect significant human activity. The
               decline of moist deciduous taxa along with non-arboreal elements in Pollen Zone GU-II suggests a shift towards
               relatively cooler and drier climatic conditions. In contrast, a subsequent increase in the abundance of Shorea robusta
               in Pollen Zone GU-III (younger zone) indicates an amelioration of climate, characterized by relatively warm and
               humid conditions than previous phase in the region.

                    Keywords: Pollen, vegetation, climate, Holocene, Assam, Northeast India.

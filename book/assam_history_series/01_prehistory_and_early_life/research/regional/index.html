Checked passages and original-report acquisition leads have different labels. This inventory covers all eight states but is not an exhaustive census or a completed factual review. Further chapter drafting remains pending.

What the comparison changes

Palaeolithic

Garo Hills older tool assignments need comparison with celt-manufacturing waste. Padhan’s reassessment questions earlier classifications. This dispute cannot be settled by resemblance alone. See NE09.

Mesolithic

Audit Selbalgiri’s claimed sequence and Manipur’s original reports. Small flakes and a Holocene age alone do not establish a microlithic Mesolithic industry. These are research tests, not chapter facts.

Neolithic

Compare excavated assemblages with surface collections. Treat tools, pottery, domesticated plants/animals and dates as separate evidence fields. A label in an old report does not prove every part of a farming economy.

Research programme before Chapter 02 onward

  1. Obtain original Palaeolithic and microlithic reports for Garo Hills, Manipur and Tripura; inspect tool drawings and sediment sections.
  2. Audit lab sample ID, material, layer, depth, uncertainty, method, calibration and association for every proposed date.
  3. Compare Daojali, Sarutaru and Marakdola with Khasi Hills, Manipur, Arunachal and Nagaland without flattening different periods.
  4. Require botanical/faunal evidence for farming claims; distinguish observed finds from inferred activity.
  5. Prepare each site dossier with location variants, excavation history, raw materials, tool technology, pottery, subsistence, burial evidence, chronology disputes and figure permissions.
  6. Recheck Chapter 01 against the expanded evidence; draft later chapters only when their divisions have locatable support.

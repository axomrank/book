# Kamarupa II — Salastambha: Rule, Society, and Bhauma Lineage Traditions

**Series position:** Book 04 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People*.

**Coverage:** Salastambha period, broadly c. seventh–tenth centuries CE; exact dates and transitions disputed.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope, names, and relationship to the set

Retain the requested Salstambha spelling as an alias and Salastambha as the working title form. Discuss Bhauma/Naraka lineage traditions explicitly, without treating Bhauma as an exclusive synonym for this dynasty. The term appears in ancestry claims across Kamarupa ruling families; see the [consulted genealogy study](https://list.indology.info/pipermail/indology/attachments/20170209/d815b926/attachment.pdf). Investigate Mlechchha source usage without imposing a modern ethnic identity.

The former combined Kamarupa plan is retained as a [superseded planning snapshot](../_superseded/02_ancient_kamarupa/README.md); use Books 03–05 as the active dynasty plans. Historical material about sacred ancestry belongs in both the mythology book and dynasty-specific analysis, with explicit cross-references rather than forced continuity.

## Audience, scale, and AxomRank identity

Write for serious general readers, students, and researchers in accessible English, preserving accurate local names and original-language forms. A commissioned Assamese edition requires full translation and fluent editorial review. Follow the [shared AxomRank editorial and visual system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **45,000–65,000 words**, excluding notes and appendices. This is a flexible estimate, not a measured manuscript or a quota for sparse evidence. The three Kamarupa books receive their own period-specific institutions, society, landscape, and material culture, rather than a list of kings with generic repeated background.

# A State Remade: Assam from Independence to the Accord

**Series position:** Book 12 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1947–1985.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

Reconstruct the post-independence state through constitutional and territorial change, social development, language politics, and the Assam Movement. The Accord text and its negotiation belong here; subsequent implementation and legal developments belong in Book 13.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **45,000–65,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Independence, constitution, territory, and language:** Chapters 1–4.
- **Development, livelihoods, plantations, and social services:** Chapters 5–8.
- **Culture, environment, migration, and politics:** Chapters 9–12.
- **Movement, violence, negotiations, and the Accord:** Chapters 13–16.

# From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar — Volume I: Origins, Foundation and Expansion

**Series position:** Book 07 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** Origins and pre-1228 context; 1228–1603 political narrative.

**Status:** Five opening chapter drafts; sixteen preliminary dossiers; incomplete and not publication-ready.

## Scope and relationship to the set

This is the existing Ahom trilogy's Volume 1 within the larger set. The user-selected title, existing chapter numbers, claims, sources, drafts, and cover decisions remain authoritative. This folder indexes and reproduces planning briefs; the [existing volume folder](../../volume_01/README.md) remains the working manuscript home. The [original Ahom master](../../Ahom_Dynasty_Book_Plan_1228_to_Gomdhar_Konwar.md) governs any discrepancy.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Use the original trilogy’s shared 220,000–300,000-word main-text target across its three volumes; do not count it three times or impose equal volume lengths.

### Chapter 01. How We Know Ahom History

**Stable planning ID:** `B07-C01`. **Division:** Sources, origins, and the early kingdom.

**Coverage:** Method and sources.

1.1. Buranjis in Tai-Ahom and Assamese: authorship, manuscript families, copying, translation, and court purposes.

1.2. Inscriptions, copper plates, coins, archaeology, diplomatic letters, religious literature, genealogies, and material culture.

1.3. Persian, Burmese, British, and other external accounts: what each can and cannot establish.

1.4. Colonial, nationalist, regional, community, and recent academic interpretations; missing voices and archival silences.

1.5. Rules for separating documented events, probable reconstructions, oral traditions, legends, and modern commemorative claims.

**Supporting material to commission:** Source-family diagram and a sample comparison of conflicting accounts.

# Assam and the Struggle for Freedom: Politics, Labour, Culture, and Partition

**Series position:** Book 11 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1905–1947, with an explicitly bounded independence transition.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

Explain public politics and the independence struggle through labour, agrarian society, culture, constitutional change, war, and Partition. The 1905 transition belongs principally here; the preceding book supplies its background. Independence introduces new questions rather than completing every community’s political project.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **45,000–65,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Reorganisation, association, mobilisation, and war:** Chapters 1–4.
- **Mass movements, labour, land, and migration:** Chapters 5–8.
- **Culture, participation, campaigns, and representation:** Chapters 9–12.
- **World war, Quit India, Partition, and independence:** Chapters 13–16.

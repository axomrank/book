# Assam and the Struggle for Freedom: Politics, Labour, Culture, and Partition

**Series position:** Book 11 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1905–1947, with an explicitly bounded independence transition.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

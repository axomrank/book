# From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar — Volume II: War, Power and Renewal

**Series position:** Book 08 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1603–1769 political narrative.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

This is the existing Ahom trilogy's Volume 2 within the larger set. The user-selected title, existing chapter numbers, claims, sources, drafts, and cover decisions remain authoritative. This folder indexes and reproduces planning briefs; the [existing volume folder](../../volume_02/README.md) remains the working manuscript home. The [original Ahom master](../../Ahom_Dynasty_Book_Plan_1228_to_Gomdhar_Konwar.md) governs any discrepancy.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Use the original trilogy’s shared 220,000–300,000-word main-text target across its three volumes; do not count it three times or impose equal volume lengths.

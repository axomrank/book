# Under Company and Crown: Colonial Rule and the Making of Modern Assam

**Series position:** Book 10 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** 1826–1905; regional incorporation dates handled individually.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

Cover the uneven establishment of colonial authority and structural change. The Ahom trilogy retains Gomdhar’s full 1828 narrative; this book provides a concise bridge and develops later resistance and restoration. Cachar and hill incorporation follow their own documentary timelines.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **45,000–65,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Conquest, administration, and uneven incorporation:** Chapters 1–4.
- **Plantations, land, extraction, and transport:** Chapters 5–8.
- **Environment, education, faith, and 1857:** Chapters 9–12.
- **Protest, province, everyday life, and public politics:** Chapters 13–16.

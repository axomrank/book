# Kamarupa: Kings, Communities, and Sacred Landscapes

**Series position:** Book 02 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** c. fourth century CE–c. 1200; earlier and later context explicitly labelled.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Priority acquisition routes

- **ARCH-SITES:** [Assam Directorate of Archaeology — protected sites and monuments](https://archaeology.assam.gov.in/information-services/detail/list-of-protected-archaeological-sites-and-monuments). Official site inventory consulted for planning; underlying excavation reports not acquired in this task.
- **ASI-AMBARI:** [ASI Guwahati Circle — Ambari excavation account](https://www.asiguwahaticircle.gov.in/excavation8.html). Official excavation summary consulted; full field records not read.
- **DHAS-CATALOGUE:** [Directorate of Historical and Antiquarian Studies — publication catalogue](https://dhas.assam.gov.in/portlet-innerpage/list-of-publications). Catalogue page inspected for planning; listed works not acquired through this page.

See the [shared source register](../../SOURCE_STARTER_REGISTER.md) for access limits. No new source downloads or field/archival research have been completed for this plan.

## Highest-impact verification priorities

1. Inscription editions and original readings.
2. Dynastic dates and disputed capital sites.
3. Davaka and early territorial claims.
4. Modern language and community labels in ancient contexts.

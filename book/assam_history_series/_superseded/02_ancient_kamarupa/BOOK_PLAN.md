# Kamarupa: Kings, Communities, and Sacred Landscapes

**Series position:** Book 02 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** c. fourth century CE–c. 1200; earlier and later context explicitly labelled.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

Follow early historical polities, material life, and the changing Kamarupa record. Names, dynasty dates, territorial reach, and cultural continuity are research questions; later legendary ancestry does not supply a dated political chronology.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **45,000–65,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Evidence, geography, and the Varmans:** Chapters 1–4.
- **Dynasties, authority, and land:** Chapters 5–8.
- **Economy, religion, language, and art:** Chapters 9–12.
- **Society, frontiers, fragmentation, and memory:** Chapters 13–16.

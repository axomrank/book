# Assam: A History of Its Land and People

**Proposed AxomRank umbrella series — thirteen books, 222 chapters, 1,110 numbered content divisions.** Revised 11 October 2026. The contemporary cutoff remains that date.

| Book | Title and dedicated folder | Coverage | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Before the Kingdoms: Prehistory and Early Life in Assam](01_prehistory_and_early_life/README.md) | Earliest recoverable evidence to c. fourth century CE; site-specific chronologies | 12 |
| 02 | [Mythological Assam: Danava, Naraka, and the Sacred Worlds of Pragjyotisha](02_mythological_assam/README.md) | Mythic narrative time, textual transmission, and later regional reception; no asserted BCE/CE reign dates | 16 |
| 03 | [Kamarupa I — Barma/Varman: Dynasty, State, and Society](03_kamarupa_varman/README.md) | Varman period, broadly c. fourth–seventh centuries CE; precise reign boundaries to verify | 16 |
| 04 | [Kamarupa II — Salastambha: Rule, Society, and Bhauma Lineage Traditions](04_kamarupa_salastambha/README.md) | Salastambha period, broadly c. seventh–tenth centuries CE; exact dates and transitions disputed | 16 |
| 05 | [Kamarupa III — Pal/Pala: Kingship, Change, and Fragmentation](05_kamarupa_pala/README.md) | Kamarupa Pala period, broadly c. tenth–twelfth centuries CE; bounded thirteenth-century transition context | 16 |
| 06 | [Many Kingdoms, Many Peoples: Regional Assam in the Medieval Age](06_regional_kingdoms_and_communities/README.md) | c. 1200–1826, with bounded regional transitions into the 1830s | 18 |
| 07 | [From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar — Volume I: Origins, Foundation and Expansion](07_ahom_origins_and_expansion/README.md) | Origins and pre-1228 context; 1228–1603 political narrative | 16 |
| 08 | [From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar — Volume II: War, Power and Renewal](08_ahom_war_and_renewal/README.md) | 1603–1769 political narrative | 18 |
| 09 | [From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar — Volume III: Society, Collapse and Resistance](09_ahom_society_collapse_resistance/README.md) | Thematic history across 1228–1828; late political narrative c. 1769–1828 | 26 |
| 10 | [Under Company and Crown: Colonial Rule and the Making of Modern Assam](10_colonial_rule_and_transformation/README.md) | 1826–1905; regional incorporation dates handled individually | 16 |
| 11 | [Assam and the Struggle for Freedom: Politics, Labour, Culture, and Partition](11_public_politics_and_freedom/README.md) | 1905–1947, with an explicitly bounded independence transition | 16 |
| 12 | [A State Remade: Assam from Independence to the Accord](12_independence_and_state_reorganisation/README.md) | 1947–1985 | 16 |
| 13 | [Assam Today in Historical Perspective: Society, Politics, and Change](13_contemporary_assam/README.md) | 1985–11 October 2026; fixed edition cutoff, not a live news feed | 20 |

Book 02 treats mythology, textual tradition, and cultural memory; its position is a reading arrangement, not a dated historical era. Books 03–05 are the three separate Kamarupa dynasty books. Book 06 is the parallel regional companion. Books 07–09 retain the existing **From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar** trilogy and its original 60 chapter numbers. Five opening Ahom chapters already have drafts; the other books remain plans.

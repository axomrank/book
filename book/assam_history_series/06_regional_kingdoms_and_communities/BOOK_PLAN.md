# Many Kingdoms, Many Peoples: Regional Assam in the Medieval Age

**Series position:** Book 06 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** c. 1200–1826, with bounded regional transitions into the 1830s.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Scope and relationship to the set

This is a parallel regional companion, not a consecutive era between Kamarupa and Ahom rule. It gives non-Ahom kingdoms, the Barak Valley, hill societies, religious networks, and community histories sustained treatment. Shared campaigns receive regional interpretation here and detailed Ahom chronology in Books 07–09; link the chapters rather than reproduce the same narrative.

## Audience, language, scale, and brand

Serious general readers, students, and researchers. Draft in accessible English with accurate Assamese and other relevant names; create a full Assamese companion edition only when commissioned, sharing research and receiving fluent editorial review. Use the [shared editorial and brand system](../EDITORIAL_AND_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

Working main-text allocation: **55,000–80,000 words**, adjusted to evidence and reader needs, excluding notes and appendices. This is a planning estimate, not a measured manuscript.

## Chapter divisions

- **Kamata, Koch, and western power:** Chapters 1–5.
- **Eastern kingdoms, Barak, and hill societies:** Chapters 6–10.
- **Religious change and cultural worlds:** Chapters 11–14.
- **Livelihoods, agency, upheaval, and regional synthesis:** Chapters 15–18.

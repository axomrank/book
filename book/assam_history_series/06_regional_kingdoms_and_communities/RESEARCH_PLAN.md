# Many Kingdoms, Many Peoples: Regional Assam in the Medieval Age

**Series position:** Book 06 of *Assam: A History of Its Land and People* (proposed umbrella title).

**Coverage:** c. 1200–1826, with bounded regional transitions into the 1830s.

**Status:** Plan only; manuscript drafting has not begun.

## Priority acquisition routes

- **DHAS-CATALOGUE:** [Directorate of Historical and Antiquarian Studies — publication catalogue](https://dhas.assam.gov.in/portlet-innerpage/list-of-publications). Catalogue page inspected for planning; listed works not acquired through this page.

Official site inventory consulted for planning; underlying excavation reports not acquired in this task.
- **STATE-ARCHIVES:** [Assam State Archives — research facilities](https://archives.assam.gov.in/portlets/research-facilities). Research-access page consulted; no archival files accessed.

See the [shared source register](../SOURCE_STARTER_REGISTER.md) for access limits. No new source downloads or field/archival research have been completed for this plan.

## Highest-impact verification priorities

1. Koch, Chutiya, Dimasa, and community source comparison.
2. Dating of hagiographies and manuscript witnesses.
3. Distinct histories of Barak and hill areas.
4. Do not turn conquest narratives into complete community histories.

Use stable book-prefixed IDs; retain all legacy Ahom IDs and note anchors in their existing folders. A catalogue or search result stays a lead until the actual passage is read. No empty register is treated as completed research.

Search in relevant languages and spelling variants. Log queries, collections, access dates, consulted passages, and unsuccessful acquisition attempts. Attribute oral testimony with consent, collection context, date, and limitations. Record source dependence, record-keeping bias, and unrepresented perspectives.

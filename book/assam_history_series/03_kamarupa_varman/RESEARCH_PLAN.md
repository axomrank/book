## Highest-impact verification priorities

1. Verify every ruler and regnal sequence against inscription editions rather than popular lists.
2. Compare Dubi and Nidhanpur genealogies, grants, and seal evidence.
3. Separate Bhaskaravarman diplomacy from later claims of durable conquest.
4. Document Barma, Barman, Varman, and Bhauma-Varman naming without implying shared ethnicity or an unbroken mythic line.

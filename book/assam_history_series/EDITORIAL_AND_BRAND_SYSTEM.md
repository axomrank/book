## Illustrations and publication proof

Prefer dated maps, authentic objects, inscriptions, facsimiles, documented photographs, genealogies, diagrams, and clear tables. Record source, date, provenance, caption, credit, and rights. Identify AI historical scenes as modern illustrations; they cannot establish the appearance of rulers, costumes, battles, or buildings.

Build editable masters and inspect final renders at intended reading size. Check hierarchy, contrast, body and footnote legibility, table overflow, clipped text, page references, image credits, map legends, and Assamese shaping. Keep source notes and bibliography intact through conversion. Separate historical review, language review, rights clearance, copyediting, and layout proofing; successful rendering alone does not make a book publication-ready.

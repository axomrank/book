# From Sukaphaa to Gomdhar Konwar

**A History of the Ahom Dynasty, 1228–1828 — an AxomRank book project.**

## Wider prehistory-to-present programme

This Ahom trilogy forms Books 07–09 of the proposed [thirteen-book Assam history series](assam_history_series/README.md). See its [master plan](assam_history_series/SERIES_MASTER_PLAN.md) for all eras and [coverage matrix](assam_history_series/COVERAGE_MATRIX.md) for regional and thematic responsibilities. The existing volume folders below remain the working manuscript homes.

Each volume has a dedicated folder containing its detailed chapter plan and structured outline.

| Volume folder | Title | Chapters | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [volume_01](volume_01/README.md) | Origins, Foundation and Expansion | 1–16 | Five opening drafts; sixteen preliminary research dossiers |
| [volume_02](volume_02/README.md) | War, Power and Renewal | 17–34 | Planned; drafting not begun |
| [volume_03](volume_03/README.md) | Society, Collapse and Resistance | 35–60 | Planned; drafting not begun |

Volume I covers origins and expansion through 1603. Volume II follows the political narrative from 1603 to 1769. Volume III includes themes across the kingdom’s history and the late political narrative through 1828.
